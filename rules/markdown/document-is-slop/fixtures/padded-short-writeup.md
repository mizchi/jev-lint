# We Improved Our Onboarding Flow

Onboarding is the first real impression a new user gets of your product, and getting it right matters more than most teams give it credit for. We recently spent some time revisiting ours and wanted to share a quick recap of what we did.

There are a lot of best practices floating around for onboarding — progressive disclosure, reducing friction, showing value early — and we tried to keep those principles in mind as we worked through our own redesign.

We simplified the signup form, cut down the number of required fields, and moved account verification to happen in the background instead of blocking the user. We also added a short guided tour highlighting the main features.

Overall, these changes led to a noticeably smoother experience, and early signup completion improved by about 15%. We're continuing to monitor how it performs and plan to keep iterating on it.

Feedback from the small group of users who tried it early was positive, and the team is excited about where this is headed. We think this lays a good foundation for future improvements to the onboarding experience.

In conclusion, small changes to onboarding can add up to a meaningful difference in how new users experience your product, and we'd encourage any team sitting on an onboarding backlog to take a closer look.
