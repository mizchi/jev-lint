# Billing system migration on October 1: what you need to know

On October 1 we are moving all customer accounts to the new billing system. This means that on October 1, every account will be migrated from the old billing system to the new billing system. Please read this notice carefully, because the migration to the new billing system happens on October 1 and there are things you need to do before then.

## Summary

- The migration to the new billing system takes place on October 1.
- Before October 1, you need to confirm your billing contact in the account settings page.
- After October 1, invoices will be issued by the new billing system.
- If you have questions, contact the billing team.

## What is happening

We are migrating to a new billing system. The migration will happen on October 1. On that date, all accounts will be moved from the old billing system to the new billing system. The old billing system will no longer issue invoices after the migration; invoices from that date will come from the new billing system.

## What you need to do

Before the migration on October 1, you need to confirm your billing contact. To confirm your billing contact, go to the account settings page and check that the billing contact is correct. This must be done before October 1, because the new billing system will send invoices to the billing contact on record at the time of the migration.

If your billing contact is correct, you do not need to do anything else. If your billing contact is not correct, update it before October 1.

## What happens after the migration

After the migration on October 1, invoices will be issued by the new billing system. The new billing system will send invoices to the billing contact you confirmed before the migration. This is why it is important to confirm your billing contact before October 1.

Your invoices will look different, because they come from the new billing system rather than the old billing system. The amounts and the billing cycle do not change; only the system that issues them changes.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the migration?**
The migration is on October 1.

**What do I need to do?**
Confirm your billing contact in the account settings page before October 1.

**Will my invoices change?**
Invoices will be issued by the new billing system after October 1. They will look different, but the amounts and the billing cycle stay the same.

**Who do I contact with questions?**
Contact the billing team.

## Recap

To recap: on October 1 we are migrating all accounts to the new billing system. Before October 1, confirm your billing contact in account settings. After October 1, invoices come from the new billing system. If you have any questions about the migration to the new billing system on October 1, contact the billing team.

Thank you for reading this notice about the billing system migration on October 1.
