# Introducing Relay 3.0: The Biggest Update Ever to Team Notifications

We are thrilled to announce Relay 3.0, the biggest update to Relay we have ever shipped. Relay 3.0 is here, and it changes everything about how your team receives notifications. Today marks the release of Relay 3.0, our most ambitious release to date, and we could not be more excited to share it with you.

Over the past several months, our team has been hard at work building the next generation of Relay. We listened to your feedback, we studied how teams actually use notifications, and we went back to the drawing board. The result is Relay 3.0, a release that we believe will transform the way your team communicates.

## Why We Built Relay 3.0

Notifications are at the heart of how modern teams work. Every day, teams rely on notifications to stay informed, stay aligned, and stay productive. But notifications can also be overwhelming. Too many notifications lead to noise, and noise leads to missed messages. We heard from countless customers that they wanted a better way to manage their notifications, and that is exactly what Relay 3.0 delivers.

With Relay 3.0, we set out to solve the notification problem once and for all. We wanted to build something that would help teams cut through the noise and focus on what matters. We wanted to give teams control over their notifications. And we wanted to make it all feel effortless.

## What's New in Relay 3.0

Relay 3.0 is packed with new features and improvements. Here are just a few of the highlights.

### Smart Grouping

Smart Grouping is one of the most requested features we have ever received, and we are delighted to finally bring it to Relay. With Smart Grouping, related notifications are automatically grouped together, so you can see everything at a glance. No more scrolling through endless lists of notifications. Smart Grouping keeps things organized so you can focus on what matters.

### Priority Channels

Priority Channels let you decide which notifications deserve your immediate attention. Simply mark a channel as a priority channel, and notifications from that channel will always rise to the top. It is a simple feature, but it makes a big difference. With Priority Channels, you will never miss an important notification again.

### A Fresh New Look

Relay 3.0 also comes with a fresh new look. We have redesigned the interface from the ground up to make it cleaner, faster, and more intuitive. Every screen has been reconsidered, every interaction has been refined. We think you are going to love it.

### Performance Improvements

Under the hood, Relay 3.0 is faster than ever. We have made significant performance improvements across the board, so everything feels snappier and more responsive. Whether you are on desktop or mobile, Relay 3.0 delivers a smooth and seamless experience.
